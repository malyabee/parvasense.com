Three tools, one investment

Most farms manage insect pressure by running three separate systems side by side: a solar-powered trap for energy-efficient field operation, a sticky trap for physical capture, and a lure trap for targeted attraction. Each is bought separately, installed separately, and maintained separately.

The ParvaSense Solar Insect Trap brings all three into a single integrated unit. One product to purchase, one point of installation, and one unit to maintain in the field — reducing the additional investment, setup effort, and upkeep that come with running three standalone products.
