Four methods, one investment

Most farms manage insect pressure by running several tools side by side: a light trap to draw insects in at night, hanging lure cans, a sticky trap to physically capture them, and no easy way to put birds to work in their favor. Each is bought separately, often made of plastic that wears out, and maintained separately.

The ParvaSense Solar Insect Trap brings all of this into a single bolt-fit iron unit — a bird perch, hanging lure trap cans, a solar blue lamp paired with a water trap, and a reusable sticky trap that never needs full replacement, only a top-up of sesame oil or water. Every part is joined with bolts and nuts, so the whole unit is easy to assemble, install, remove, and maintain.

Why ParvaSense is different

Four field-tested methods, bolted together in a single reusable iron housing.

Method 1: Bird Perch

A T-shaped perch at the top gives birds a natural landing spot near your crop, encouraging free, natural predation of insects.

Method 2: Lure Trap Cans

Iron lure cans hang at the edges of the bird perch. Their scent and colour draw insects in, trapping them away from your crop.

Method 3: Solar Blue Lamp Trap

A solar-powered blue lamp lights up at night and draws insects to it. The insects fall into the water-filled can right below the lamp and stay trapped — simple, and nothing to plug in.

Method 4: Reusable Sticky Trap

The iron can body is coated with sesame oil to create a sticky trapping surface. When it loses stickiness, just reapply sesame oil and reuse it — no plastic sheet to throw away and rebuy.

Four systems, one bolt-fit housing

The bird perch, lure trap cans, solar blue lamp with water trap, and reusable sticky surface all bolt onto a single iron body and a single install point in the field.
